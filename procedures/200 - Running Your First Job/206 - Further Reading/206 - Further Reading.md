## Intro to PACE ICE

[Open the Intro to PACE ICE guide](https://github.com/guru-desh/Intro-To-PACE-ICE)

This community guide covers topics such as:

- Connecting to ICE
- Storage and file transfers
- Conda and Python environments
- Interactive jobs
- CPU and GPU batch jobs
- Job arrays
- Slurm commands
- Troubleshooting

A good place to continue is the **Running Jobs on PACE** section. You can also use the Slurm and PACE command cheatsheets as references while working.

> This is a community-maintained guide, so some commands, quotas, GPU details, or scheduling examples may become outdated. Check the official PACE documentation before copying a resource request or submitting a large job.
