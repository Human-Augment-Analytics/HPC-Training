## Your first milestone

After ICE access is approved, work through Modules 100 and 200. You should be able to connect, submit a small Slurm job, and inspect its expected output. [Open the first-job walkthrough]({{ '/procedures/200%20-%20Running%20Your%20First%20Job/205%20-%20Submitting%20Your%20First%20Job/205%20-%20Submitting%20Your%20First%20Job.html' | relative_url }}).

This curriculum was assembled in Summer 2026 and has not yet been piloted with independent learners. Technical details may change; verify current cluster policy and resources with [PACE](https://pace.gatech.edu/) before relying on them.
